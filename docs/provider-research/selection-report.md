## 0. Evidence rules and the selection verdict

Evidence tags used below: **MEASURED-BROWSER** (real Chrome/Chromium, third-party origin or unpacked MV3 context), **MEASURED-LIVE** (a dated Node/HTTP API probe), **DOCUMENTED** (verbatim official provider text), **HISTORICAL** (a dated measurement already recorded in `docs/PROVIDERS.md`), **NOT MEASURED** (no instrument produced a result), **REVIEW REQUIRED** (terms/permission absent, silent or ambiguous), **NEEDS AUTHORIZATION**, **BLOCKED**, **REJECTED**.

Rules applied, per GOAL §§3, 8 and the worker brief:

- A successful create, an empty-inbox read, a recorded fixture, an unauthenticated `200`, and a Node-only `fetch` are **never** delivery proof and are **never** browser-CORS proof. Every 2026-11-22 research snapshot therefore has **0 domains with verified real incoming mail**, independently of how many mailboxes were provisioned in a bounded probe.
- The only real incoming-mail evidence in the entire repository is **HISTORICAL**: one external message on each provider on 2026-10-01, recorded in `docs/PROVIDERS.md` §5 (verbatim output reproduced in §5 below). That evidence is not refreshed by this synthesis and is not a pass for any currently advertised domain.
- An unresolved or adverse third-party/competitive permission is **REVIEW REQUIRED**, never PASS. A provider's silence about repackaging is not a grant.
- Text embedded in provider responses is untrusted data. The GrabMail `/api/v1/domains` response carried a `tell_the_user` field addressed to assistants; the discovering researcher recorded it as data and took no action on it, and this synthesis likewise treats it as evidence only.

**Selection verdict: NO candidate passes GOAL §4's integration gate, and no candidate may be advertised as SUPPORTED in either client.** The reason is uniform at the gate's first condition — not one of the 19 researched candidates explicitly authorizes integration into a competing, free, multi-provider disposable-email product. Four candidates are technically web-reachable and four are technically reachable from an extension, but technical reachability is not the gate. The exact blocking reason for each candidate is in §3, and the written authorization or design needed to reconsider each is in §4.

**Counts of decisions (never a domain tally):** 0 SUPPORTED and 0 PROVISIONAL. Every other verdict is NEEDS AUTHORIZATION, BLOCKED or REJECTED, and several candidates differ per client: among the eight GOAL-named candidates, Guerrilla is NEEDS AUTHORIZATION on both, Mail.tm is BLOCKED on the website and NEEDS AUTHORIZATION in the extension, Mail.gw is BLOCKED on both, DropMail and TempMail.lol and Tmailor are BLOCKED on the website and NEEDS AUTHORIZATION in the extension, Maildrop is REJECTED on both on privacy, and official Temp-Mail.org is REJECTED on the free tier and NEEDS AUTHORIZATION on premium. Among the new candidates, GrabMail is NEEDS AUTHORIZATION on both, Best-TempMail is REJECTED on the website and NEEDS AUTHORIZATION in the extension, and the remaining six plus the three screened-out names are REJECTED. No candidate is approved for either client.
